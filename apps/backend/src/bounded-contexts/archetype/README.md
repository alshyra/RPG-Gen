# Archetype

Archetype are also known as classes, we use it to avoid the dnd terminology and class keyword.
In the game it's a concept linked to lore and storytelling.
Each archetype has its own characteristics, strengths, and weaknesses.

## Available Archetypes

- **Warrior**: A strong and resilient fighter, excelling in melee combat.
- **Mage**: A master of magical arts, capable of casting powerful spells.
- **Rogue**: A stealthy and agile character, skilled in sneaking and surprise attacks.

## Specializations and talent trees

Each archetype can have multiple specializations, allowing players to customize their characters further.
